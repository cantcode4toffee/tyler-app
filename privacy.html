Tyler App privacy notice

The Tyler App is a private dashboard used by one family (the Tylers) for their own household. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With the account holder's own approval, it reads account names and balances from the family's own bank accounts (Monzo, and NatWest through Enable Banking). Transaction history may later be read to spot regular bills.

Where the data goes

Bank access keys stay on the family's home PC. Balances are copied into the family's own private Google spreadsheet, which only family members can open. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no analytics or tracking are used.

How long it is kept

Balances are overwritten each time they update. Bank access can be withdrawn at any time from the bank's app, and it ends on its own after at most 180 days.
