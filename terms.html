Tyler App terms

The Tyler App is a private household dashboard for the Tyler family only. It is not a public service and has no other users.

It only reads balances (and, later, transactions) from the family's own accounts. It cannot move money or make payments.

It is provided as is, with no charge. The family can stop bank access at any time from the bank's app. See the privacy notice for what is read and where it goes.
